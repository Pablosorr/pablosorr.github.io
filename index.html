Analytical mechanics · Numerical simulation

Coupled systems and nonlinear dynamics

With Rodrigo Vercher Lidón

Modelling of several coupled-oscillator systems, a double pendulum and masses joined by springs, deriving their equations with the Lagrangian formalism and solving them numerically to analyse the transition from regular to chaotic motion. On my own initiative, beyond the assignment, I extended the study to a double pendulum with elastic rods, where I identified a 2:1 Fermi resonance between the angular and radial motion.

Experimental design · Signal analysis

Inertially coupled oscillators: avoided crossing

With Alejandro Lozano

I designed a lab experiment from start to finish instead of following a given script: an oscillator with purely inertial coupling, a pendulum hanging from a spring-mounted cart. I predicted the resonance length theoretically before measuring, built the setup and analysed the motion with Tracker and Fourier transforms, observing an avoided crossing between the normal frequencies of the system, a phenomenon also found in quantum mechanics.

Model and data agree within 2.5 % at the minimum gap · 8 coupling lengths

End-to-end experimental design Theoretical prediction Tracker FFT

Metrology · Statistical analysis

Measuring g with a Kater pendulum

With Alejandro Lozano

Experimental measurement of local gravity with a reversible Kater pendulum, obtaining the value by linear regression on the oscillation periods. Beyond the result, I analysed the source of the discrepancy with the reference value and showed, from a study of the residuals, that the error is systematic and random in nature, not a flaw of the method.

High-quality fit: R² = 0.998 · g = 9.0 ± 0.4 m/s²

Regression and fitting Uncertainty analysis Error treatment Critical reasoning
